# Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Projects

## 1. Customer Lifecycle Analytics - HDFC Bank

### Objective
Optimize customer journey from onboarding to digital adoption.

### Analysis Components

#### Customer Segmentation
- Behavioral segmentation based on transaction patterns
- Lifecycle stage classification (Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Growth, Churn)
- Risk propensity scoring

#### Engagement Metrics
- Feature adoption tracking across digital channels
- User engagement scoring
- Satisfaction and NPS analytics

#### Predictive Insights
- Churn probability by customer segment
- Engagement propensity models
- Cross-sell and upsell opportunity identification

### Key Dashboards Built
1. **Customer Health Dashboard** - Real-time engagement metrics
2. **Churn Risk Dashboard** - Early warning system for at-risk customers
3. **Feature Adoption Dashboard** - Digital channel performance tracking
4. **Campaign Performance Dashboard** - A/B test results and ROI tracking

### Business Impact
- 📈 **52% increase** in digital feature adoption
- 📊 **34% improvement** in click-through rates (CTR)
- 😊 **25% improvement** in customer satisfaction scores
- 💰 Estimated incremental revenue impact: ₹100M+
